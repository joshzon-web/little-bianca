Where our wines come from

  1. Piedmont Barolo, Barbera, Gavi, Langhe Arneis
  2. Lombardy Lugana
  3. Veneto & Friuli Prosecco, Valpolicella, Amarone
  4. Emilia-Romagna Lambrusco, our house Sanleo wines
  5. Tuscany Chianti, Brunello, Vin Santo
  6. Marche Pecorino
  7. Abruzzo Montepulciano d’Abruzzo
  8. Campania Greco di Tufo
  9. Puglia Primitivo, Negroamaro, Falanghina
  10. Sicily Grillo, Nero d’Avola, Chardonnay
  11. Sardinia Cannonau

Our wines are grouped by style, then by region, moving from the north of Italy down to the south, before a small selection from the rest of the world.

Sparkling

Northern Italy

  • Lambrusco Emilia IGT Ogni Giorno30.00

    A gently fizzing, dry red sparkling wine from Emilia-Romagna — violets and dark cherry, the everyday toast of the region.

  • ●Prosecco DOC Brut Tenuta Ca’ Vescovo32.00

    Crisp Veneto prosecco with pear and green apple, a fine persistent mousse and a clean, appetite-sharpening finish.

Champagne & Rest Of World

  • Carte Blanche Brut Champagne, Charles Ellner75.00

    A grower’s Champagne from the Marne valley — biscuity and refined, with citrus zest and a long, chalky finish.

  • Moët & Chandon Impérial Brut NV95.00

    The house style of Épernay: apple and citrus fruit lifted by fine bubbles and a soft brioche note.

  • Veuve Clicquot Yellow Label Brut NV105.00

    Full-bodied and generous, with ripe orchard fruit and a toasty, confident finish — the Widow’s signature blend.

  • Dom Pérignon Vintage280.00

    A single vintage of exceptional chardonnay and pinot noir — layered, precise and built to age. Champagne at its most reflective.

White

Northern Italy

  • ●Pinot Grigio Friuli DOC Zonin Classici31.00

    Pale, dry and delicately floral, with citrus and a touch of almond — classic Friuli pinot grigio.

  • ●Sauvignon Friuli DOC Aquileia Ca’ Bolani47.00

    Zesty and herbaceous, with gooseberry and fresh-cut grass — an easy-drinking sauvignon from Friuli.

  • Aquilis Sauvignon Friuli DOC Aquileia Ca’ Bolani53.00

    Aromatic and more concentrated, with white peach and elderflower — a serious expression of Friulian sauvignon.

  • Gavi del Comune di Gavi DOCG Cossetti43.00

    Piedmont’s cortese grape at its best — bone dry, mineral and lemony, with a touch of almond on the finish.

  • Langhe DOC Arneis Damilano47.00

    A Piedmont white of pear and white flowers, softly rounded with a gentle almond note.

  • Lugana DOC Zonin Jewels49.00

    Grown on the shores of Lake Garda, Lugana is fresh and saline with ripe citrus and a mineral edge.

Central Italy

  • Pecorino Falerio DOC CasalFarneto35.00

    A Marche white with real character — ripe stone fruit, herbs and a savoury, saline finish.

  • Viognier Maremma Toscana DOC RDM37.00

    Perfumed and generous, with apricot and honeysuckle from the warm Tuscan coast of the Maremma.

  • Calasole Vermentino Maremma Toscana DOC Rocca Montemassi41.00

    Coastal Tuscan vermentino — bright citrus, herbs and a whisper of the sea.

Southern Italy

  • ●Sanleo Bianco Borgo SanLeo25.00

    Our house white: light, dry and easy-drinking, with soft citrus and a clean finish.

  • Grillo Sicilia DOC Zonin Classici27.00

    Sicily’s own grape — ripe tropical fruit and citrus blossom with a warm, sun-soaked character.

  • Chardonnay Ventiterre29.00

    An unoaked Sicilian chardonnay, all orchard fruit and gentle roundness, made for easy drinking.

  • Chardonnay Sicilia DOC Feudo Principi di Butera39.00

    Riper and fuller than its Ventiterre cousin, with baked apple, vanilla and a creamy texture.

  • Falanghina Salento IGT Masseria Altemura45.00

    Puglia’s aromatic white — white flowers, pear and a lively, refreshing finish.

  • Greco di Tufo DOCG Tenuta del Meriggio80.00

    One of Campania’s noble whites — structured and mineral, with white pepper and stone fruit.

Rest Of World

  • Picpoul de Pinet, Domaine De La Madone33.00

    A Languedoc classic grown almost within sight of the sea — searingly fresh, citrus-driven and made for shellfish.

  • Pouilly Fuissé Grand Vin de Bourgogne, M. A. Geoffroy60.00

    Burgundian chardonnay at its most refined — white peach, hazelnut and a long, creamy finish.

  • Sea Change Pacific Trebbiano-Pinot Grigio45.00

    A crisp, easy-drinking blend from Australia. Sea Change helps fund ocean conservation, having raised over €600,000 for marine projects, and bottles it with minimised packaging to reduce its footprint.
